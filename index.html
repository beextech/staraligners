Digital dentistry, built with precision.

StarAligners is a dental technology company. We design and produce custom clear aligners for dentists, orthodontists and clinics, working from the lab side of the industry.

Every case starts as data. We plan it digitally, design each aligner stage by stage, and produce it with the same care from the first set to the last.

We don't treat patients. We support the professionals who do, with precise work, clear communication and dependable timing.

From scan to delivery in six steps.

Each case moves through the same digital workflow. You stay in control of the plan. We take care of design and production.

01

Digital case

You send the intraoral scan and your clinical notes. We work with data from major intraoral scanners, including iTero, 3Shape and Medit, as well as scanned physical models.

02

3D planning

We build a 3D treatment plan from your scan, in consultation with you. You review, refine and approve every step.

03

Aligner design

The approved plan is translated into aligner designs, one for each stage of treatment.

04

Production

Each aligner set is produced from the approved digital design, with consistency from the first set to the last.

05

Quality control

Every set is checked against the digital plan before it leaves production.

06

Delivery

Production and delivery are completed within 72 hours of plan approval.

Questions from clinics.

How does a clinic start working with StarAligners?

Send a quote request, or message us on WhatsApp or by email. We set you up on our portal and send your starter scanning kit within the first day.

How much do StarAligners aligners cost?

Pricing depends on case complexity and the number of sets. Send us your first scan and we'll reply with a quote within 24 hours.

Which scanning systems do you accept?

We work with all major intraoral systems, including iTero, 3Shape and Medit, as well as scanned physical models.

How long does the first delivery take?

Once the 3D plan is approved, production and delivery are completed within 72 hours, regardless of how many sets the case needs.

What happens if a refinement is needed?

Refinements are planned and produced at no extra cost, covered by the guarantee included with every case.
